# Superstore-Sales-Performance
## Project Objective 
The objective of this project is to analyze retail sales, quantity, profit, and shipping performance across different regions, customer segments, and product categories from 2014 to 2017 to support data-driven business decisions.

- <a href="https://github.com/shrutikini872004/Superstore-Sales-Performance/blob/main/Sample%20-%20Superstore.csv">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the overall Sales, Quantity, Profit, and Average Delivery Days?
- Which customer segment contributes the highest share of sales?
- Which region generates the highest profit?
- How do monthly sales and profit trend across different years (2014–2017)?
- Which product categories and sub-categories drive the most revenue?
- Which shipping mode is most preferred by customers?
- How are sales and profit distributed across different states?

## Process
- Verify data for any missing values, duplicates, and anomalies, and clean the same in Power Query.
- Make sure data is consistent and clean with respect to data types, date formats, and currency values used.
- Create DAX measures and build visuals (cards, donut charts, line/area charts, bar charts, and map) according to the business questions asked.
- Merge all visuals into one interactive dashboard and apply regional slicers (Central, East, South, West) to make it dynamic.

## Project Insights
- **Overall Performance:** Total sales reached **2.3M** with **38K** units sold, generating **286.4K** in profit and an average delivery time of **4 days**.
- **Top Customer Segment:** The **Consumer** segment leads sales with **51%**, followed by Corporate (**31%**) and Home Office (**19%**).
- **Regional Profit:** The **West** region generates the highest profit (**38%**), while the **Central** region lags behind (**14%**).
- **Seasonality:** Monthly sales and profit peak consistently between **September and December** every year.
- **Top Categories & Sub-Categories:** **Technology (0.84M)** is the top-selling category, with **Phones (0.33M)** and **Chairs (0.33M)** leading sub-category sales.
- **Shipping Mode:** **Standard Class (0.52M)** is the most heavily used shipping method.

## Final Conclusion
- Focus on increasing inventory for high-demand sub-categories like **Phones** and **Chairs** ahead of Q4 (September–December) to maximize holiday revenue.
- Investigate pricing, discounting, and shipping costs in the **Central** region to improve its low profit contribution (**14%**).
- Develop targeted B2B marketing strategies to grow market share in the **Corporate** and **Home Office** segments.
